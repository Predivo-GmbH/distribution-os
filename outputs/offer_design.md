# Phase 2B — Offer Design: LaunchReady

> Completed: 10 March 2026

## Pricing Tiers

### Free — "Quick Check"
- **Price:** $0
- **What you get:**
  - 1 audit per month
  - Overall score (0-100) + category scores
  - Top 5 issues listed (title only, no fix code)
  - No AI-generated fixes
  - No guided walkthroughs
  - LaunchReady branding on results page
- **Purpose:** Lead magnet. Users see their score, see the red items, and want the fixes.

### Starter — "Fix It"
- **Price:** $19/month (or $9 one-time per audit)
- **What you get:**
  - Unlimited audits
  - Full scored checklist (all checks, pass/fail)
  - AI-generated fix code for every failed check (copy-paste ready)
  - Plain-English explanations for every fix
  - Guided walkthroughs (Search Console, Google Business Profile, social debuggers)
  - Re-audit on demand (check if your fixes worked)
  - Email summary of results
- **Purpose:** Core revenue tier. Covers the "just launched" persona who needs fixes once.
- **One-time option:** $9 per audit makes this a no-brainer impulse purchase. "Your $5,000 SEO audit for $9."

### Pro — "Stay Ready"
- **Price:** $39/month
- **What you get:**
  - Everything in Starter
  - Weekly automated re-audits with change detection ("Your Lighthouse score dropped from 95 to 82")
  - Email/Slack alerts when something breaks (OG image 404, sitemap missing, score regression)
  - White-label PDF report (download/share with clients or stakeholders)
  - Priority audit queue (results in <30 seconds)
  - Audit history + trend charts (score over time)
  - Up to 5 websites
- **Purpose:** Recurring revenue from web developers and small agencies who want ongoing monitoring.

## The Irresistible Offer

**Hook sentence:**
> "Your website is live. Google doesn't know it exists. Fix that in 60 seconds — for free."

**Secondary hook (for paid conversion):**
> "Agencies charge $2,000-5,000 for a post-launch SEO audit. LaunchReady does it for $9, with copy-paste code you can implement in 10 minutes."

**The free-to-paid bridge:**
The free tier shows your score and lists your problems. The paid tier shows the solutions. This is the classic "show the pain, sell the cure" model — and it works because the pain is real (you can see your 43/100 score) and the cure is instant (copy-paste code).

## Objection Busters

### Objection 1: "I can just use Google Lighthouse for free"
**Response:** Lighthouse gives you scores and developer-level recommendations. LaunchReady gives you the actual code to paste. Lighthouse says "Document does not have a meta description." LaunchReady says "Here's your meta description, based on your page content: `<meta name='description' content='...'>`  — paste it in your `<head>` tag." That's the difference between a report and a solution.

### Objection 2: "I already use Semrush/Ahrefs"
**Response:** Those are $140-500/mo enterprise tools for SEO professionals. If you're a solo founder who just launched ONE website, you don't need keyword rank tracking, backlink analysis, and 47 other features you'll never use. You need to know: is my site ready for Google? LaunchReady answers that one question for $9.

### Objection 3: "Can't I just follow a blog checklist?"
**Response:** You can. It'll take you 4-6 hours to research each item, figure out the correct code, test it, and verify it works. Or you can paste your URL and get everything in 60 seconds with code ready to copy. Your time is worth more than $9.

## Launch Pricing Strategy

### Phase 1: Founding Member Rate (first 100 users)
- **Starter:** $9/month (locked for life) — normally $19/month
- **Pro:** $19/month (locked for life) — normally $39/month
- **Messaging:** "First 100 users get founding member pricing — locked forever. No price increases, ever."

### Phase 2: Early Bird (users 101-500)
- **Starter:** $14/month
- **Pro:** $29/month

### Phase 3: Standard Pricing
- **Starter:** $19/month (or $9 one-time per audit)
- **Pro:** $39/month

### Alternative: One-Time Audit Model
If subscription fatigue is a concern, offer a **$9 one-time audit** as the primary low-friction entry point. Users who want monitoring naturally upgrade to Pro ($39/mo). This could be the dominant revenue model if the audience skews toward "fix it once and move on."
