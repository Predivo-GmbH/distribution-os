# Phase 1 — Idea Validation: Post-Launch Website Audit & Fix Tool

> Completed: 10 March 2026

## Winning Idea

A web app where users paste their website URL and get an automated audit + AI-generated fix code + guided walkthroughs for everything a new website needs after launch (SEO meta tags, Lighthouse fixes, Search Console setup, sitemap, structured data, social sharing).

## Origin

Built from the exact 13-step post-launch workflow executed manually for predivo.ch (9-10 March 2026). Every check, fix, and edge case comes from production experience.

## Market Size

- 250K-1M+ new websites created per day
- Web design services market growing at 4.8% CAGR (2025-2032)
- Every new site needs SEO setup, most creators are non-technical

## Pain Point Evidence

- Semrush: 48% 5-star vs 36% 1-star on Trustpilot — power users love it, beginners hate it
- "You don't know what you don't know" is the dominant theme in small business SEO
- Every "website launch checklist" search result is a blog post, not a product
- No tool under $99/mo generates copy-paste fix code for the "just launched" persona

## Target Persona

Freelancer, small business owner, or indie maker who:
- Just launched a website (any platform, not just WordPress)
- Knows SEO matters but finds enterprise tools overwhelming and expensive
- Wants exact fixes with code to paste, not a 58-item checklist article
- Needs guided walkthroughs for Search Console, Google Business Profile, etc.

## Verdict: VALIDATED

Clear gap, real demand, uncontested price point ($9-29/mo).
